Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Stillroom is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What Stillroom accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Stillroom asks for permission to:

  • read and update the titles and thumbnails of videos on the owner's channel (YouTube Data API), and
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports.

How the data is used

Stillroom uses this access only to run the owner's thumbnail and title tests: changing a video's title or thumbnail at scheduled times, restoring the original afterwards, and showing the owner the analytics for each option.

Where the data is kept

Sign-in tokens, test settings and analytics are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and in services the owner controls. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Stillroom at any time in its Settings, or remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stillroom's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
